About

CJ Flemings is a Montreal recording artist known as the Soft Spoken Prince — hip-hop-infused pop marked by crisp delivery over post-trap drums and haunting melodies. Through Soft Spoken Co. he has released a catalog of singles and albums from 2015 through 2022, including Forever Wanted More and Mascara Tears, after years of mixing and mastering his own. This site is the home for his music and updates.
